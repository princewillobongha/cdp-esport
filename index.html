01 / ABOUT US

Built around the community.

Comrades Democratic Party (CDP ESPORT) is presented as an African gaming community focused on bringing gamers, creators, streamers and esports competitors together.

The community begins with a strong Nigerian base and a vision to grow through neighboring countries before expanding its network across Africa.

◎
MISSION

Unify Gamers

Connect gaming communities and create opportunities for players, creators and competitive talent.

◈
VISION

One African Gaming Community

Build a recognizable, connected esports community that grows from Nigeria to countries across Africa.

✦
VALUES

Family & Opportunity

Support creators, discover grassroots talent and encourage collaboration across gaming communities.
